# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Crest is used by crypto market analysts and active traders who need to scan volume-ranked assets, chain strength, sector rotation, 4h regimes, and 30m setup triggers inside one dense workspace.

## Product Purpose

Crest turns live market snapshots into a disciplined terminal for regime tracking, liquidity rotation, and context-aware AI market review. Success means the user can trust the latest available data, inspect the reasoning behind setups, and ask AI questions without leaving the analytical flow.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, operational.

## Anti-references

Avoid retail crypto styling, marketing dashboards, oversized cards, gradient decoration, crypto-bro iconography, chat-bubble AI, and generic SaaS settings pages.

## Design Principles

- Data first: typography, alignment, and timestamps carry trust.
- Color is signal: semantic states and market meaning only.
- Dense but readable: analyst workflows should stay compact without hiding detail.
- Admin surfaces are operator consoles: configuration must feel controlled, testable, and auditable.
- AI answers must be bound to available Crest data, never vague external guesses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast dark terminal surfaces, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, readable monospace data, reduced-motion support, and copy that describes exactly what an action does.
